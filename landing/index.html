Questions, answered

What is Nudge Deck?

Nudge Deck is a playful couples game for iOS. You and your partner share a Deck of Nudges — little challenges, dares, questions, and things to do together — then take turns sending, responding, and nudging back.

Who is Nudge Deck for?

Nudge Deck is built for couples. You pair with one partner using an invite code and play together for a season.

Who can see my proof photos and voice notes?

Proof you attach to a Nudge is visible to your paired partner. It is not shown to other users.

How do I sign in?

Nudge Deck uses Sign in with Apple to create and secure your account.

What are quiet hours?

Quiet hours let you pause delivery of new Nudges overnight or during focus time. Nudges your partner sends during quiet hours are held and delivered when quiet hours end.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email hello@nudgedeck.app to request account and data deletion. We will confirm when the deletion is complete.
